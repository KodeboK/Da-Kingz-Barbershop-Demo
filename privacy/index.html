PRIVACY.

This site saves your color theme preference on this device. It collects aggregate page views, link and feature clicks, referring domains and broad device categories to understand site usage. The site keeps these counts for 90 days and does not use visitor identifiers for these measurements. It respects Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals.

The map only loads when you choose it. Google Maps, Google Calendar and Instagram have their own privacy practices. The administrator area uses a secure session cookie. Short-lived hashed network identifiers help rate-limit abuse; these records expire automatically. No customer booking information or payment details are collected by the sample calendar. Appointment providers handle information submitted on their own websites.
